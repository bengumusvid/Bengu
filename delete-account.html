Delete your account

In the app

  1. Open Bengu and log in.
  2. Go to Menu → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. This cannot be undone.

By email

If you cannot log in, email getbengu@gmail.com from the address registered on your account with the subject “Delete my account”.

What is deleted

Your profile and the content you published are deleted. Some records may be kept where required by law or to prevent abuse.
