Privacy Policy

Bengu is a social network where you post photos, videos, music and text, chat with friends, and follow news, tournaments and competitions. This policy explains what we collect and why. The person responsible for Bengu is Horácio Eduardo (getbengu@gmail.com).

1. What we collect

  • Account data: name, username, email or phone number, country, age (to protect minors), and your password (stored securely by our authentication provider).
  • Content you publish: posts, photos, videos, music, comments, messages, statuses and reels.
  • Payment-related details you enter for contests, votes or withdrawals (for example your M-Pesa or PayPal reference). Payments are processed by the payment providers.
  • Basic technical data needed to run the app, such as language, device and browser type, and view counts.

2. How we use it

To run the app, show your content to other users, deliver chats and notifications, run competitions and tournaments, prevent abuse, protect minors, and show ads. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Service providers

  • Hosting, database and file storage: Supabase.
  • Advertising: Google AdSense, which may use cookies or similar technologies.
  • Automatic translation: when your language is not English or Portuguese, some interface text is sent to online translation services (Google Translate or MyMemory). Your private messages are not sent for translation.
  • Sports data: TheSportsDB.

4. Children

You must be at least 13 to use Bengu. For children under 13, an adult must create the account together with them. Accounts of users under 18 have limited messages and lives.

5. Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile, choose who can download your posts, delete your content, and delete your account in Settings. You can also ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing getbengu@gmail.com. See how to delete your account.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical measures, but no online service is perfectly secure. Keep your password safe: if you forget it, the account may not be recoverable.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the bottom shows the latest version.
